# Perudo

An online, private-room version of Perudo, the South American bluffing dice game, for 2–6 players.

## Dice

**Face**:
The value showing on a die, from 1 to 6.
_Avoid_: Value, number, pip

**Paco**:
A die showing face 1. Pacos are wild, except during a Palifico round.
_Avoid_: Ace, one, joker

## Bidding

**Bid**:
A claim that at least a given count of dice, across all players, show a given face.
_Avoid_: Call, guess, auction

**Raise**:
A bid that legally outbids the current bid.
_Avoid_: Outbid, overbid

**Dudo**:
Declaring that the current bid is too high, which ends the round and reveals all dice.
_Avoid_: Challenge, doubt, call

**Calza**:
Declaring that the current bid is exactly right, which ends the round and reveals all dice.
_Avoid_: Exact call, spot-on

**Palifico**:
A special round, opened by a player who has just dropped from two dice to one, in which Pacos are not wild and the face of the opening bid is locked.
_Avoid_: Palafico, one-die round

## Structure

**Room**:
A private space that players join by link, where games are played.
_Avoid_: Lobby, table, server

**Host**:
The player in a room who sets its options and starts games and rounds.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin, creator

**Game**:
One match inside a room, played until a single player has dice left.
_Avoid_: Match, session

**Round**:
The span from a roll of the dice until someone loses or gains a die.
_Avoid_: Hand

**Turn**:
A single player's single action within a round: a bid, a Dudo or a Calza.
_Avoid_: Move, play

**Seat**:
A player's fixed position in a game's turn order.
_Avoid_: Slot, position

**Opener**:
The player who makes the first bid of a round.
_Avoid_: Starter, first player

**Eliminated**:
The state of a player who has lost their last die and takes no further part in the game.
_Avoid_: Out, dead, knocked out
